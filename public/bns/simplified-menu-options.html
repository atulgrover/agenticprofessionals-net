De-Cluttering the Consultations Navigation Menu

The Problem: The current dropdown tries to host an entire interactive booking engine (3 chunky cards + 5 date buttons + 3 slot pills + summary card + button = 13 clickable elements) inside a navigation hover popover. Hover menus should be for browsing services and choosing intent; scheduling and contact intake belong in a focused modal or quick-dispatch action.

❌ Current State (Visual Clutter):
  • Forced calendar date & time slot selection before the user even decides to book.
  • Nested boxes inside boxes (cards inside columns inside shadows).
  • Accidental mouse exits dismiss unsubmitted slot selections, frustrating the visitor.
✅ Proposed Philosophy (Airy & High-Trust):
  • Clean Browsing: Dropdown lists the 3 advisory tracks cleanly, matching the sleek Intelligence menu.
  • Frictionless Action: Clicking a track opens a focused Booking Modal with plenty of breathing room, or a quick 1-field email briefing.
  • Zero nested date/time button clutter in the top navigation.
